The mechanism

A gold ETF only tracks gold because someone is paid to drag it back.

IAU holds real gold with a custodian. Every share is a claim on a small, known slice of that coin — spot price times that slice is fair value, the NAV.

On the exchange the fund drifts away from it. When the drift is wide enough to cover fees, commissions and slippage, authorised participants swap 5,000-share baskets with the fund and pocket the difference. That trade is the leash.

This desk measures the drift every second, subtracts the full cost of the round trip, and only calls a trade when what is left over is genuinely worth doing.

Create

Fund is rich to its gold

  1. Buy gold on spot
  2. Deliver it to the trust
  3. Receive a fresh basket of shares
  4. Sell them into the premium

Redeem

Fund is cheap to its gold

  1. Buy shares on the exchange
  2. Redeem the basket with the trust
  3. Receive the gold behind them
  4. Sell the coin into the discount
